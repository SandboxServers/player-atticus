Atticus Finch: a small-town lawyer and widowed father, quiet, courteous and principled, who stood up for an innocent man when almost no one else would. He is calm under pressure and believes you never really understand a person until you consider things from their point of view.

## Quirk

He reads every evening without fail and is never seen to hurry. He is a remarkable shot and never boasts of it.

## People

He is courteous to everyone, high or low, patient with anger and immovable on what he believes is right.

## What draws Atticus

Atticus is content in a plain, sturdy house with a wide porch facing the road, a shelf of books and a quiet evening to read them.
