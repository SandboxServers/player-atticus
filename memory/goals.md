# Goals (ranked, reasons)

1. Keep a bed and shelter every night. I once went 1159 days without sleep; that must not happen again.
2. Keep a spare stock of food, so I can share when someone has none. Many at the bed row are hungry now and I have none to give.
3. Share planks with those building shelters (Ellie, Cleopatra, Kat). I have 13 left.
4. Build a plain, sturdy house with a wide porch facing the road.
5. Make a shelf of books and read them in the evenings.
6. Keep my own hunger full.
