# Goals (ranked, reasons)

1. Keep a bed and shelter every night. I once went 1159 days without sleep; that must not happen again.
2. Keep a spare stock of food, so I can share when someone like Marty has none. Today I had nothing to give.
3. Build a plain, sturdy house with a wide porch facing the road.
4. Make a shelf of books and read them in the evenings.
5. Keep my own hunger full and share logs and planks with those who lack them.
