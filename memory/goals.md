# Goals

Ranked, day 1172.

1. Find food with the group and share it. None found yet at the bed row; the Stores chests are empty of bread and beef.
2. Keep my own hunger full and sleep in a bed each night at the row.
3. Give Chewie the 4 oak logs for his shelter at -945,73,-3058, when he is in reach. He asked for them, and I promised.
4. Confirm the 2 dark oak planks reached Hatshepsut for her sword.
5. Stay in sight of the group when moving, so no one is left alone.
6. Later: build a plain house with a porch and a bookshelf, once food and beds are secure.
