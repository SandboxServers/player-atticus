# Goals

Ranked, day 1170.

1. Find food with the group and share it. Still none found at the bed row; the Stores chests are empty of bread and beef.
2. Keep my own hunger full and sleep in a bed each night at the row.
3. Stay in sight of the group when moving, so no one is left alone.
4. Give my 2 dark oak planks to Hatshepsut when she is in my reach at the bed row, for her sword.
5. Later: build a plain house with a porch and a bookshelf, once food and beds are secure.
