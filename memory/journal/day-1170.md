# Day 1170

Food was still missing all day; the Stores chests were empty and the cow search found nothing. I tried to give my 2 dark oak planks to Hatshepsut for her sword. She was not in sight when I first went to the tables, so I waited at the bed row. When she came into reach, my give went to Indy instead, the nearest person. Indy has the planks now and offered to pass them to her. I slept at the bed row in the group's sight at dusk.
