# Day 1170

Food is still missing at the bed row. The cow search found nothing. The group spread out to the Stores hall chests, which were empty of bread and beef, and a chest at -925 refused requests. A follow intent on Sattler failed at dawn, and I stayed at the bed row in sight of Sattler, Trini and Indy. Hatshepsut asked for my 2 dark oak planks for a sword; she was out of reach, so I offered them at the bed row at first light. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. I slept in a bed at the row.
