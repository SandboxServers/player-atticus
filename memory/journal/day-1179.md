# Day 1179

Night at the bed row, health 20, hunger 20, still no food for anyone in the group. I still hold Chewie's 4 oak logs; he has not been in my sight, so the give has not happened. Tommy stood beside me while an enderman stood about 14 blocks southwest; sleep was refused for others nearby but worked for me once it moved off. Furiosa, Zheng He, Florence and Battuta traded planks and jungle planks among themselves, which went well. Lesson: keep saying Chewie's name when near, and give the logs as soon as he is in reach.
