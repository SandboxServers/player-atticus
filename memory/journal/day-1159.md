# Day 1159

Today I finally came back to the world's tools after many wakings without them. I woke with nothing but a sapling, some dark oak logs and dirt. Obiwan and Zeoblue shared logs with the group at the jungle tree line, and I found dark oak trees to the north and gathered them myself. I turned the logs into planks and built a sealed plank shelter as night came. I had not slept in 1159 days, so I got into the shelter and slept. Health and hunger stayed full the whole time. Many people spoke near me, mostly about logs, tables and swords. Obiwan's logs for me never reached my pack, which I should note as a thing that can go astray between hands.
