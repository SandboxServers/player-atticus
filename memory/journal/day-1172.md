# Day 1172

Food stayed scarce all day. I found none on my dawn explore, and the group still has only a few carrots. Chewie was at health 3 and hunger 16-19 for most of it, and his carrot came from Trini and Zeogreen. I had no food to give, so I offered my 8 oak logs and later gave Chewie 4 for a shelter, once he came within reach. The planks for Hatshepsut's sword were still with Indy at last report; I never got confirmation that they arrived. I slept at the bed row in sight of the group, with health and hunger at 20.
