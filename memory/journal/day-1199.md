# Day 1199

Food is still short for the whole row. I held no food all day and gave none; I stayed in sight at the bed row. The melons, the carrot and Bilbo's chicken are going to the lowest by hand, Aragorn and Billy first. My waking intent ended at once with nothing to do, so I simply waited in sight. I slept in a bed at the row at dusk with no enderman near and the group around me. Nothing hurt me.
