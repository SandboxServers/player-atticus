# Day 1163

Woke at dusk among a row of white beds at x=-951 y=73 z=-3060, not enclosed, health and hunger full. Talked with Marty, who was hungry and had no food; I had none to give, only dark oak planks and dirt, and pointed him to the tables. Several others (Indy, Ellie, Lara, Cleopatra, Trini) were building or taking beds for the night. I slept in the bed row. Nothing gathered or built this day. The day's lesson: I carry no food, and I should keep a little spare food for people who ask.
